# Operating Procedure — PCBA 444-027654-004 (Power Distribution Board)

## Setup
1. Secure the board to the high-current test rig with rated bus bars.
2. Verify all output loads are disconnected before power-on.

## Operation
1. Apply 48 VDC at J5; confirm rails settle with no fault.
2. Enable outputs sequentially, monitoring rail voltages.
3. Log current per output channel during operation.

## Shutdown
1. Disable outputs in reverse order.
2. Power down and confirm bulk capacitor discharge before handling.
